# Code of Conduct — qisas-studio

> ملخص عربي: كن لطيفًا ومحترمًا؛ لا إهانة ولا تحرش ولا تمييز ولا محتوى
> مسيء؛ واحترم أصحاب القصص والجمهور. من يخالف يُنبَّه أولًا، ثم يُحظر
> عند التكرار. النص الإنجليزي أدناه هو المعتمد.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in qisas-studio a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful — especially to new contributors.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users, not on winning
  an argument.
- Showing empathy toward other community members, including the people
  whose real stories inspire the videos this project produces.
- Writing clearly and concretely: reproduce the error, name the command,
  paste the exact output.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, baiting, or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing anyone's private information (address, phone number,
  email, API keys, tokens) — in an issue, a PR, or a comment.
- Posting content that is hateful, violent, or demeaning toward any
  person or group.
- Using the project to copy, republish, or redistribute someone else's
  video content without their permission.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions) and in any public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

- **First violation:** a private warning from a maintainer, explaining
  which behavior broke this code and what to do instead.
- **Repeat or serious violation:** a temporary or permanent ban from the
  project spaces, at the maintainers' discretion.
- Maintainers will act on every report they can verify, and will keep
  the reporter's identity private.

## Reporting

To report a violation, open a **private** report: use GitHub's
"Report a vulnerability" flow on this repository only for security
issues (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). For conduct issues, contact a
maintainer through a GitHub issue marked `[conduct]` **without naming
any private details** — a maintainer will follow up privately.
